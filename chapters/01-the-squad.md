# The Squad

A [[Squad]] consists of **5 [[Trooper|Troopers]]**. At the beginning of the game, name the Squad and the Troopers. 

## The Sergeant

One Trooper is the Squad's **Sergeant**. They fight like anyone else, but they read the ground a half-second faster. Spend **1 [[Grit]]** to nullify an [[Enemy Tactics|Enemy Tactic]]. The Sergeant cannot do this while [[Bleeding Out]] or [[Suppressed]].

## Gear

A Trooper carries **[[Armor]]**, a **[[Weapon]]**, and any **Special Gear**: Special Weapons and Special Equipment. Gear is what makes one Trooper different from another. It is also what slows them down. See *Equipment & Gear*.

It is generally smart to have a Medic on your Squad (i.e. someone with Medic Gear) and a Radio Operator (someone with Radio Gear).

## Grit

An expendable resource that allows a Trooper to push through. Spend 1 Grit to reroll any d6 the Trooper rolls for themselves: A **[[Mobility Check]]**, a **[[Defense Roll]]** (reroll 1d6 of the pool) or a **[[Death Save]]**.

Troopers start with 1 Grit (out of 1 max. Grit). Capacity rises by 1 for every mission the Trooper survives. Grit refills to Capacity between missions.

Grit can be recovered in the field when:

- **A [[Hard Target]] goes down.** One Trooper of your choice regains 1 Grit.
- **The [[Offense Roll]] runs hot.** Each 6 rolled beyond the +2 [[Momentum]] cap either removes 1 [[Pressure]] or grants 1 Grit to a Trooper of choice.

## Ammo

An abstraction of munitions and supplies carried. A Trooper starts a mission with 3 Ammo, and can hold a maximum of 3. Spend it to fire Special Weapons or to add +1 ATK. Other rules call for Ammo as well: declaring an [[Assault Posture|Assault]], [[Pour It On|pouring it on]]. Find more in the field, or draw from a Supply Backpack out of contact.

## Mobility 

The Trooper's ability to maneuver and haul ass. Base value is **4**, lowered by selected armor, weapons & gear. Mobility is the target number for every **Mobility Check**. Roll 1d6. Equal or under passes. It also sets the **[[Flanking Bonus]]**:

| Mobility  | Flanking Bonus |
| --------- | -------------- |
| 5 or more | +3 ATK         |
| 4         | +2 ATK         |
| 3 or less | +1 ATK         |

## Injuries

Troopers have no hit points. They accumulate **Injuries**, and Injuries alone do not stop a Trooper fighting. Each 2 Injuries lower Mobility by 1.

Each Injury makes the next one harder to walk off. When a Trooper is hit, they add the Injuries to their tally and roll a **[[Death Save]]**: 1d6, beat the new total. A 6 always holds. On a failure the Trooper is **[[Bleeding Out]]** and out of the fight until someone drags them clear.

How many Injuries a hit deals, when a Death Save is called for, and what becomes of a Bleeding Out Trooper are all covered in *Engagement*. Injuries clear slowly. See *After the Mission*.

## Tags

Give each Trooper one **Tag**. It describes how they approach a problem when the problem is not a firefight.

- **Forceful.** Physical solutions. Kicks doors, carries wounded, holds the line through brute effort. Intimidates when talking fails.
- **Technical.** Systems and logic. Hacks terminals, disarms devices, reads schematics, operates unfamiliar equipment.
- **Steady.** Composure and patience. Waits out a tense situation, resists interrogation, holds position for hours, talks down a panicking civilian.
- **Sharp.** Reads people and situations. Notices the detail others miss, fast-talks past a checkpoint, picks up that something is wrong before anyone else does.

A Tag answers the **Skill** question when a single Trooper takes on a task. Read it broadly: would this kind of person approach this problem with confidence?

> [!tip] First game?
> Keep your Squad nice and simple: come up with 5 Trooper names and give them each an **[[Assault Rifle]]**, **[[Medium Armor]]** (Mobility 4, Flanking Bonus +2 ATK), 1 Grit, 3 Ammo. Play a simple [[Mission]] with a single Sector ([[Dense Cover]], [[Open Space]], [[Threat Level]] 1), and jump ahead to the [[Advance]] to start!
