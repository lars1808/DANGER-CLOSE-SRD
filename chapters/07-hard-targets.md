# Hard Targets & Attached Forces

An optional method to further raise the stakes is by introducing **Hard Targets**. These can be mobile armored machines such as tanks, or entrenched static positions such as a gun nest.

Hard Targets must be targeted separately during the [[Fire]] action. Some equipment, such as rocket launchers, provide a clear and distinct advantage against Hard Targets.

When Firing upon a Hard Target, set those d6 aside in a separate pool and roll them individually **before** the [[Offense Roll]]. 

Momentum cannot pass BREAKING THROUGH while a Hard Target holds the field. Momentum gain still applies up to that point and its other effects resolve as normal: Pressure drops, positioning shifts, 6s beyond the cap remove Pressure or grant Grit. The Engagement does not end. Allocate accordingly.

- A **4-6** deals 1 Hit. 
- A **2-3** deals 1 Hit at a Cost: one Trooper takes **-1 DEF** during the [[Defense Roll]]. You can decline the cost and treat it as a miss.
- A **1** does not deal damage.

Interpret/reflavor Hard Targets at will; a Brute can be a bipedal armored walker, a hulking monstrosity, or just a very big guy with a very big gun.

## Attached Forces

The Squad can be accompanied by additional fighters, escorts, or hangers-on. Assign them a bonus based on rough size: **+1 ATK** for a few, **+2 ATK** for a sizable group, **+3 ATK** for a large one.

Attached forces don't participate/aid/hinder during [[Contact Roll|Contact Rolls]], hanging back to let the Squad make their move. 

During any [[Exchange]], the Squad can choose to commit some or all of their dice to the [[Offense Roll]]. Roll the committed dice alongside the Squad's, but keep them separate. Any committed die that comes up 1 is removed, representing casualties. When all dice are removed, the attached force is gone.

**VIPs**. When escorting a VIP, the VIP is always the last die in the group. They can't be targeted separately, but if committed to an Exchange and the last die rolls a 1, the VIP is killed.
